🚗 PDRides Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2025

1. Introduction

PDRides ("we," "our," or "us") is a community carpooling service that connects drivers and passengers through WhatsApp. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following information:

  • Phone Number: Your WhatsApp phone number is used to identify your account and communicate with you about rides.
  • Name: You may provide your name during onboarding to personalize your experience.
  • Ride Information: Details about rides you offer or join, including pickup/destination locations, date, time, and number of seats.
  • Location Data: Addresses you provide for ride matching purposes. We do not track your real-time location.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Connect you with other users for carpooling purposes
  • Facilitate ride matching and booking
  • Send you notifications about ride requests, confirmations, and updates
  • Maintain your ride history and preferences
  • Improve our services and user experience

4. Information Sharing

We share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • With Other Users: When you join a ride, the driver receives your phone number to coordinate pickup. When you offer a ride, passengers can see your phone number to contact you.
  • Service Providers: We may share information with service providers who help us operate our platform (e.g., hosting services).
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • Secure data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Regular security reviews and updates

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services. You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

6.1 Account and Data Deletion

You have the right to delete your account and personal data at any time. However, please note the following:

  • 60-Day Retention Period: After you request account deletion, your personal data will be retained in our database for approximately 60 days. This retention period is maintained for legal and government compliance purposes, including resolving disputes, enforcing our agreements, and protecting our legal rights.
  • What Happens During Deletion: During this 60-day period, your account will be deactivated and you will not be able to log in or use our services. However, your data will still be accessible to us for the purposes stated above.
  • Automatic Deletion: After the 60-day period, all your personal data, including your name, phone number, ride history, saved routes, and preferences will be permanently deleted from our active database.
  • Cancellation During Retention: If you change your mind during the 60-day period, you can cancel the deletion request by logging back into your account. This will restore your account and data immediately.
  • How to Request Deletion: You can request account deletion through the Settings page in the app, or by contacting our support team via WhatsApp or email.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt-out of certain data processing

8. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
