1. Introduction

PDRides ("we," "our," or "us") is a community carpooling service that connects drivers and passengers through WhatsApp and Telegram. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your personal data when you use our Platform. By using PDRides, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

2.1 Personal Information

  • Phone Number: Your WhatsApp phone number is used to identify your account, communicate with you about rides, and send verification codes (OTP).
  • Name: You may provide your name during onboarding to personalize your experience.
  • Email Address: Optional, for account recovery and notifications.

2.2 Ride Information

  • Details about rides you offer or join, including:
    • Pickup and destination locations/addresses
    • Date and time of rides
    • Number of seats available or requested
    • Contribution amount (fare)
  • Ride status (active, full, completed, cancelled)
  • Passenger information (for drivers)

2.3 Location Data

  • Addresses you provide for ride matching purposes
  • Geographic coordinates (latitude/longitude) for pickup and destination
  • Important: We do NOT track your real-time location

2.4 Driver-Specific Information

  • Vehicle information (make, model, year, color, license plate)
  • Driver's license status
  • Vehicle insurance information
  • Driver verification status
  • Total trips completed

2.5 User Preferences

  • Notification preferences (ride requests, updates, promotions)
  • Communication preferences (WhatsApp, SMS, email)
  • Privacy settings (profile visibility, phone visibility)
  • Driver preferences (smoking, music, chat, pets, food, phone charging)

2.6 Usage and Activity Data

  • Ride history and search history
  • Ratings and reviews you give or receive
  • Session tokens and login history
  • Account activity timestamps

3. How We Collect Your Information

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Directly from you: When you register, create a ride, search for rides, or communicate with other users
  • Automatically: When you use our Platform (session data, cookies, usage analytics)
  • From third parties: WhatsApp and Telegram APIs (message metadata, user identifiers)

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • Account Management: Create and maintain your account, verify your phone number via OTP
  • Ride Matching: Connect you with other users for carpooling purposes
  • Communication: Send notifications about ride requests, confirmations, and updates via WhatsApp and Telegram
  • Service Improvement: Analyze usage patterns to improve our services and user experience
  • Security: Detect and prevent fraud, unauthorized access, and other illegal activities
  • Legal Compliance: Comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests
  • Customer Support: Respond to your inquiries and provide assistance

5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We share your information only in the following circumstances:

5.1 With Other Users

  • When you join a ride: The driver receives your phone number to coordinate pickup
  • When you offer a ride: Passengers can see your phone number to contact you
  • Profile information: Name, rating, vehicle information (for drivers), and ride history may be visible to other users

5.2 With Service Providers

We may share information with service providers who help us operate our platform:

  • Cloud hosting services (MongoDB Atlas)
  • WhatsApp Business API (Meta)
  • Telegram Bot API
  • Google Maps Platform

5.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information:

  • If required by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities
  • To enforce our Terms of Service
  • To protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of company assets
We do NOT sell your personal information to third parties. Your data is only shared as described in this policy.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information:

  • Secure Transmission: All data is transmitted using HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Access Controls: Authentication requirements and role-based access
  • Data Encryption: Sensitive data is encrypted at rest
  • Regular Security Reviews: We regularly review and update our security practices
  • Session Management: Session tokens expire after 7 days of inactivity
  • Rate Limiting: Protection against brute-force attacks on OTP

8. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services.

8.1 Active Account

  • Your personal information is retained while your account is active
  • Ride history is maintained for account functionality

8.2 Account Deletion

You have the right to delete your account and personal data at any time. However, please note the following:

  • 60-Day Retention Period: After you request account deletion, your personal data will be retained in our database for approximately 60 days. This retention period is maintained for:
    • Legal and government compliance
    • Resolving disputes
    • Enforcing our agreements
    • Protecting our legal rights
  • During Deletion Period: Your account will be deactivated and you will not be able to log in or use our services. However, your data will still be accessible to us for the purposes stated above.
  • Permanent Deletion: After the 60-day period, all your personal data, including:
    • Name and phone number
    • Ride history
    • Saved routes and preferences
    • Ratings and reviews
    will be permanently deleted from our active database.
  • Cancellation During Retention: If you change your mind during the 60-day period, you can cancel the deletion request by logging back into your account. This will restore your account and data immediately.
  • How to Request Deletion: You can request account deletion through the Settings page in the app, or by contacting our support team via WhatsApp, Telegram, or email.
