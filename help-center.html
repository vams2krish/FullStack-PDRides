❓ Help Center

👤 Account & Profile
❓ How do I edit my profile information?
▼

To edit your profile information:

  • Go to the Settings page from the main menu
  • Tap on "Edit Profile"
  • Update your name, email, or emergency contact information
  • Tap "Save Changes" to apply your updates
🔑 How do I change my password?
▼

To change your account password:

  • Navigate to Settings
  • Tap on "Change Password"
  • Enter your current password and your new password
  • Confirm the new password and tap "Change Password"

Note: If you're using WhatsApp authentication, password changes are optional but recommended for added security.

🚗 How do I update my vehicle information?
▼

To update your vehicle information (for drivers):

  • Go to Settings and switch to Driver mode
  • Tap on "Vehicle Information"
  • Update your vehicle make, model, year, color, and license plate
  • Tap "Save Vehicle" to update
🗑️ How do I delete my account?
▼

To delete your account:

  • Go to Settings
  • Scroll down and tap on "Delete Account"
  • Read the information about what happens when you delete your account
  • Confirm by typing "DELETE" in the confirmation field
  • Tap "Delete My Account"

Important: After deletion, your data will be retained for 60 days for legal and government compliance purposes. You can reactivate your account by logging back in during this period.

🔒 What are the privacy settings available?
▼

PDRides offers various privacy settings:

  • Show Profile: Control whether your profile is visible to other users
  • Show Phone Number: Choose if your phone number is visible to drivers/passengers
  • Show Rating: Control visibility of your rating score
  • Show Ride History: Decide if others can see your past rides
  • Allow Messages: Enable/disable receiving messages from other users
  • Show Online Status: Choose if you're shown as online

Find these settings in Settings → Privacy

🚗 Rides & Carpooling
🔍 How do I find a ride?
▼

Finding a ride is easy:

  • Open the app and go to the Map
  • Enter your pickup location and destination
  • Browse available rides that match your route
  • Select a ride and tap "Join Ride"
  • Coordinate pickup details with the driver via WhatsApp
➕ How do I offer a ride?
▼

To offer a ride to other passengers:

  • Switch to Driver mode in the app
  • Enter your pickup location and destination
  • Set your departure date and time
  • Specify the number of seats available
  • Set your contribution amount (fare)
  • Tap "Offer Ride" to publish
⚙️ What are driving preferences?
▼

Driving preferences allow you to set your ride style:

  • Smoking: Allow or prohibit smoking in your vehicle
  • Music: Preference for playing music during rides
  • Chat: How much you like to chat with passengers
  • Pets: Allow or prohibit pets in your vehicle
  • Food: Allow or prohibit food in the vehicle
  • Phone Charging: Offer phone charging to passengers

Configure these in Settings → Driving Preferences

💳 Payments & Pricing
💰 How is the fare calculated?
▼

PDRides uses distance-based pricing:

  • Base Fare: $5.00 for the first mile
  • Under 5 Miles: $5 + (miles - 1)
  • 5-10 Miles: $5 + miles
  • 10-15 Miles: $5 + 1.1 × miles
  • 15-20 Miles: $5 + 1.2 × miles
  • Above 20 Miles: $5 + 1.3 × miles

Tips for drivers are optional and separate from the fare.

🤝 How do payments work?
▼

PDRides facilitates rides through direct coordination:

  • Fares are agreed upon before the ride
  • Payment is typically made directly to the driver (cash or transfer)
  • The contribution amount shown is the suggested fare
  • Tips are optional and can be given directly to drivers
🛡️ Safety & Trust
⭐ How do ratings work?
▼

Our rating system builds trust in the community:

  • After each ride, both drivers and passengers can rate each other
  • Ratings are on a scale of 1-5 stars
  • Your rating is calculated from all your ratings
  • You can view a user's rating before joining their ride
  • Low-rated users may have limited access to the platform
🚨 What should I do in case of an emergency?
▼

In case of emergency during a ride:

  • Ensure your personal safety first
  • Contact local emergency services (911) if needed
  • Report the incident through the app or contact support
  • Document any relevant information for reference

Emergency Contact: We recommend adding an emergency contact in your profile settings.

📱 How is my data protected?
▼

We take data privacy seriously:

  • Your personal information is encrypted and stored securely
  • We only share necessary information with other users for ride coordination
  • You can control your privacy settings at any time
  • You can request to delete your account and data at any time
  • After deletion, some data may be retained for up to 60 days for legal compliance

See our Privacy Policy for more details.

🔧 Technical Support
📱 How do I verify my account?
▼

Account verification is done through WhatsApp:

  • Enter your phone number during registration
  • You'll receive a verification code via WhatsApp
  • Enter the code in the app to verify your account
  • Verification helps build trust in the community
💬 How does WhatsApp integration work?
▼

WhatsApp is used for communication:

  • After booking a ride, you'll be connected with the driver/passengers via WhatsApp
  • Coordinate pickup location, timing, and any special requests
  • Receive ride updates and notifications through WhatsApp
  • Our WhatsApp bot can help with common questions
🔄 What if I'm not receiving verification codes?
▼

If you're not receiving codes:

  • Check your internet connection
  • Ensure your phone number is correct with country code
  • Wait a few minutes and try again
  • Check if you have blocked unknown numbers
  • Contact support if the issue persists
