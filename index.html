Pay only for the distance you travel

📍 First Mile (Base Fare) $5.00
🚗 Under 5 Miles $5 + (miles - 1)
🛣️ 5 - 10 Miles $5 + miles
🛤️ 10 - 15 Miles $5 + 1.1 × miles
🛣️ 15 - 20 Miles $5 + 1.2 × miles
🛣️ Above 20 Miles $5 + 1.3 × miles
