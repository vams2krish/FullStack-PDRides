7. Fare Structure

Our pricing is transparent and distance-based:

  • First mile (Base Fare): $5.00 flat
  • Under 5 miles: $5 + (miles - 1)
  • 5 - 10 miles: $5 + miles
  • 10 - 15 miles: $5 + 1.1 × miles
  • 15 - 20 miles: $5 + 1.2 × miles
  • Above 20 miles: $5 + 1.3 × miles
Tips: Drivers may receive tips from passengers, which are optional and separate from the fare. PDRides does not collect any platform fees.
